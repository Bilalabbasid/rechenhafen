# Duplicate Intent & Cannibalization Decisions

Audit conducted across all candidate duplicate and overlapping calculator groups on RechenHafen.de. Every decision prioritizes clear German search intent, consolidates link equity where intents are identical, and sharply differentiates valid distinct intents.
