## What each merged calculator gained

**Finance**
- **Ratenkreditrechner:** works out the monthly rate *or* the term. Optional down payment, balloon (Schlussrate) and yearly extra repayments. Shows total interest, total cost, a yearly repayment plan, and Soll/effektiv rate conversion.
- **Tilgungsrechner:**
  - Fixed initial repayment or Volltilger (rate for a full payoff within the Zinsbindung).
  - Loan derived from purchase price, Nebenkosten and equity, including 100 %/110 % financing.
  - Restschuld at the end of the Zinsbindung, total payoff time and repayment plan.
- **Sparrechner:**
  - Solves for Endkapital, the monthly saving needed, or the time to reach a goal.
  - Shows the Sparquote from net income.
  - Dynamik and inflation apply in Endkapital mode.
- **Vorfälligkeitsentschädigung:** Baufinanzierung or Ratenkredit. For Ratenkredit it applies the § 502 BGB cap (1 % or 0.5 %, and never more than the remaining interest), checked against the law text.

**Tax and salary**
- **Brutto-Netto:**
  - Every employee contribution listed separately: KV including Zusatzbeitrag, PV, RV, AV.
  - Optional employer share with levies (Umlagen) and total employer cost.
  - Estimated refund when church tax is claimed as a Sonderausgabe.
- **Einkommensteuer:** Soli with Freigrenze and Milderungszone, plus a mode that starts from a known amount of income tax. Church tax included.

**Car**
- **Spritkosten:** optional Maut, parking and wear, trips per month, cost per person.
- **Kraftstoffverbrauch:** new conversion mode between l/100 km, km/l, MPG US and MPG UK.
- **Dienstwagen:** compares the logbook method with the 1 % rule. Cost cap (Kostendeckelung); commute counted both ways.

**Maths and geometry**
- **Dreieck:**
  - Starts from three sides, from base and height, or from two sides and the angle between them.
  - Shows area, perimeter, all three heights, angles, inradius and circumradius, and the triangle type.
  - Checks the triangle inequality.
- **Prozentrechner:** Prozentwert, Prozentsatz, Grundwert, add or subtract a percentage, and backward modes.
- **Quadratwurzel:** any root n, exact simplification (√72 = 6·√2), and odd roots of negative numbers.
- **Kreis:** starts from radius, diameter, circumference or area.
- **Dezimal ↔ Bruch:** works both ways, including repeating decimals.
- **Prozentuale Veränderung / Unterschied:** shows both, with Prozentpunkte where they apply.
- **Binär/Hex/Dezimal:** input in any base, plus arithmetic and bitwise operations and hex ↔ RGB colour codes.
- **Längen-Umrechner:** includes miles and nautical miles.

**Date and time**
- **Tagerechner:** days, weeks plus days, and months plus days (calendar-correct), plus a countdown to a time of day.
- **Tage bis Geburtstag:** weekday of birth, age at the next birthday, next round birthday, and 29 February handling.
- **Datum plus/minus Tage:** a switch between adding and subtracting days.
- **Arbeitstage:** Werktage (Monday to Saturday) by Bundesland.

**Health and family**
- **BMI:** healthy weight range, Creff ideal weight, and an age note.
- **Kalorienbedarf:**
  - Grundumsatz by Mifflin, revised Harris-Benedict, or a measured value.
  - Then PAL and the goal (lose, maintain or gain weight).
- **Puls-Trainingszonen:** HFmax by Tanaka, 220 − age, or a measured value. Zones by % of HFmax or Karvonen.
- **WHR:** adds waist-to-height ratio, WHO and NICE bands.
- **Zyklusrechner:**
  - Fertile window and a 3-cycle calendar.
  - Irregular cycles; ovulation dated by the standard cycle-day convention.
- **Erstausstattung Baby:** diaper budget for 12, 24 or 36 months, with a cloth-diaper comparison.

**Household, business and kitchen**
- **Stromkosten Geräte:**
  - Presets for TV, fridge (EU label kWh/year), air conditioning and standby.
  - Fridge comparison: old appliance against a new one.
- **Rentabilität:** Umsatz-, Eigen- and Gesamtkapitalrentabilität, with the leverage effect.
- **Esslöffel/Teelöffel/Gramm:** plus grams ↔ millilitres by ingredient density.
- **Mietbudget:** checks a concrete rent (Mietbelastungsquote) against the budget.
- **Betonrechner:** volume with reserve, then fertigbeton bags, self-mix (cement, gravel, water) or ready-mix volume.

## Deliberately kept separate

Each pair was compared on inputs, outputs and GSC queries:
- **Interior vs. facade paint:** different inputs (room vs. facade) and different searches.
- **Hohlzylinder vs. Zylinder:** pipe weight by material is a different search intent.
- **Auto-Wertverlust vs. Auto-Gesamtkosten:** a depreciation curve vs. running costs.
- **Proteinbedarf vs. Makronährstoffe:** body weight vs. a calorie target as input.
- **Backform vs. Portionsrechner:** scaling by pan size vs. by number of servings.
- **Brutto-Netto vs. Netto-Brutto:** separate high-volume searches; both have impressions.
- **Zinseszins vs. Sparrechner:** a one-off sum vs. regular deposits.
- **Alter in Tagen, Wochen and Monaten vs. Altersrechner:** each ranks on its own query at positions 5–16.
- **Waschmaschine, Spülmaschine and Trockner** vs. the device electricity calculator: each ranks at positions 9–14 with its own impressions.

Kept despite zero impressions, because other pages depend on them:
- `teilzeit-gehaltsrechner`, `sondertilgungsrechner` and `cups-in-gramm-rechner`: each is the main tool of a Ratgeber article.
- `indexmiete-rechner`: linked from the Mieterhöhung result.

## Corrections made along the way

- **Soli Freigrenze 2025** corrected from 18.720 € to 19.950 € (married couples 39.900 €), as raised by the Steuerfortentwicklungsgesetz.
- **E-company car:** the 0,25 % rule now applies up to a 100.000 € list price, for cars acquired from 1 July 2025 (before that, 70.000 €). Above the limit the calculator applies 0,5 % and shows a notice.
- **Altersrechner and other date callers:** no more negative days in month-end cases. For example, 31.01. → 01.03. now gives 1 Monat 1 Tag.
- **Result notice box:** it was labelled "Gesetzlicher Hinweis (§ 4 ArbZG)" on every calculator. It now reads "Hinweis", except on the working-hours calculator.
- **PV Kinderabschlag:** the 1 % floor was removed, so 5 or more children now give 0,8 %.
- **Dienstwagen logbook comparison:** the commute is now counted both ways.
- **Broken links fixed:**
  - two Ratgeber articles used category URLs that don't exist;
  - the Kies-Splitt page linked a non-existent Schotter calculator;
  - 12 links in page text pointed at retired calculators.
- **Removed invented figures:** a flat "650 € Notar-Ersparnis" and a made-up risk surcharge.
